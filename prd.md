Product Name

MediGuard

Vision

Build an AI healthcare companion that remembers a patient's entire medication journey and proactively prevents medication errors while improving long-term health management.

Problem Statement

Medication errors remain one of the leading preventable causes of patient harm because medical information is fragmented across hospitals, pharmacies, and providers. Existing medication reminder apps only notify users when to take medicine, while patient portals merely store records. Patients often struggle to recognize dangerous drug interactions, identify medication-related symptoms, and organize their health history for doctor visits.

MediGuard addresses this challenge by providing an AI-powered healthcare platform with persistent memory, enabling proactive medication safety, intelligent symptom analysis, and personalized health assistance.
